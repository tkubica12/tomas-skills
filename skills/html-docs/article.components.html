Reference · sheet components · page one

One page.
Everything a decision needs.

A sheet is authored for skimming on paper: a kicker, a claim-style title, a short lead, then evidence blocks and a closing line.

  • 4blocks in a two-column grid
  • 1accent shared with every view
  • 7.5 ptsmallest allowed text, for notes

Claims as
block titles

Each block title states a result. The paragraph gives its reason in two to four sentences, with the one phrase a skimming reader must catch in bold.1

Qualifications
stay visible

A condition that changes the conclusion belongs in the block, not only in a note. Notes hold sources, dates, and definitions.

Authored, not
generated

The sheet is a third depth. Write it for paper; do not copy card bodies or slide cues into it.

Fits or
gets shorter

Text never shrinks to fit. When a page overflows, cut words or move a block to another page.

Recommended actions

  • Use a sheet for a one-pager, datasheet, or leave-behind brief.
  • Pair it with an article when readers also need the full reasoning.
  • Export the PDF and inspect every page before sending it.

Paper rewards fewer, stronger claims.
